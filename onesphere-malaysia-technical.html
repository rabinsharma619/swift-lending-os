OneSphere Malaysia, as an engineering build.

The SNPL lending business built on finOS — architecture (C4 + kernel), the Malaysia solution topology (Ria, DuitNow, the KPKT pack), the delivery timeline, and the engineering organisation.

The platform architecture is reused from the corridor programme; everything Malaysia-specific — the regulatory pack, the rails, data residency — is called out. The go-live is gated by the KPKT licence, not by engineering.

1 · Architecture overview

finOS — the platform stack

finOS is a standalone lending platform. OneSphere Malaysia is a client configuration of the SNPL product, which runs on the finOS lending core and its supporting components — both reused from the corridor programme, not rebuilt for Malaysia.

2.3 · The credit kernel & one ledger

A single transactional boundary — one PostgreSQL, ACID. A balance is only ever the sum of credit_operation entries (D4). Rails interact via SAGA at the edge, never inside the kernel. This is what makes the 18% cap enforceable and every decision replayable.

3 · Solution topology

The SNPL request, end to end

The remittance is disbursed by Ria as remitter of record (keeping OneSphere out of money transmission); repayment lands via DuitNow AutoDebit as a PAYMENT operation allocated by the servicing waterfall.

Regulatory-pack architecture

The core stays market-agnostic; jurisdiction rules live in versioned packs. regpack-malaysia sits alongside NRB/SEBON/FCA — a new pack, not a fork. Guardrails run in a Guardrail SPI outside policy scripts (D10): the 18% cap as a hard rate-ceiling clamp, the s.23 fee prohibition in the product schema, the s.27 attestation as a required, audited condition before disbursement, and the citizenship rule as a keyed prohibition.

7 · Risks & critical path

Stated openly, with mitigations

RiskTypeMitigation
KPKT declines online lending to work-permit holdersRegulatory · criticalAsk in writing at G0; hold scope; fallback citizen/PR segment.
Licence issuance / online approval slips > 1 yearDelivery · highLicence track is the critical path; drive full issue now.
s.27 attestation cannot be satisfied remotelyRegulatory · highReverse-engineer an approved Schedule J; confirm with KPKT before UX freeze.
Rate cap uneconomic (fees illegal, s.23)Commercial · highSNPL at larger tickets; confirm flat-vs-reducing at G0.
Remittance leg triggers MSB licensingRegulatory · highRia = remitter of record; counsel + BNM view before freeze.
Data residency blocks Kathmandu processingArchitecture · highMalaysia-resident data plane; masked non-prod data.
Ledger correctness defect reaches productionEngineering · severeProperty-based invariants from Phase 0; daily reconciliation tripwire; every correction operation-sourced (D4).
Migrant flight-risk (borrower leaves Malaysia)Credit · highTenure ≤ remaining permit validity; permit-expiry early warning; real write-off policy.

Critical path: Phase 0 (four questions) → conditional approval → build + MYNIC → full approval → pilot (conventional licence issued 20 Nov 2026). Engineering runs in parallel and is not the binding constraint.
