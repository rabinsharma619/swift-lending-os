Who it's for

One lending OS, whatever kind of lender you are

The same kernel and modules serve every shape of lending business. What changes is configuration: the products, the regulatory pack, and which records finOS keeps. A bank with a core banking system and a moneylender with no systems at all run the same code — they just switch on different parts of it.

Four ways to connect — the letter on each card

  • AMode A: Standalone · no core banking system
  • BMode B: GL-only · books in your core or ERP
  • CMode C: Full mirror · money stays in your core
  • DMode D: Origination only · your core services the loan

Banks

C/D

Retail, SME and corporate lending

A bank already keeps customer money and its general ledger in a core banking system. finOS adds what that core is weakest at — fast origination, explainable decisions, collections — and either mirrors each loan into the core (C) or hands approved loans to it and keeps origination only (D).

Usual mode
C or D
Typical products
Personal and home loans · SME and corporate facilities · deposit-backed loans
finOS keeps
The loan (C), or the application and decision (D). Money and books stay in your core.
Leans on
Core banking port · collateral · covenants · maker-checker

Finance & hire-purchase companies

A

Vehicle and equipment finance, term loans

No core bank sits behind a finance company, so finOS is the whole system: the loan book, the cash and the company’s own books. Dealers originate, field officers collect, and an asset can be repossessed and resold — all inside one record.

Usual mode
A
Typical products
Vehicle and equipment hire purchase · term loans · dealer-originated loans · flat or reducing-balance interest
finOS keeps
Everything — loans, cash, tills and cheques, the general ledger and statements.
Leans on
Accounting · dealer portal · field app · repossession

MFIs & cooperatives

A

Group and individual micro-loans

Field-first lending with full books. Group and joint-liability loans and member loans run on the same kernel as any other product; member savings stay in your savings system and integrate rather than being rebuilt.

Usual mode
A
Typical products
Group and joint-liability loans · individual micro-loans · member loans
finOS keeps
Loans, cash and the general ledger. Member savings stay in your savings system.
Leans on
Accounting · field app · collections

Licensed moneylenders & digital lenders

A

Short-tenure personal and salary-linked loans

App-first lending inside a statutory rate ceiling. The regulator’s caps and rules sit in the regulatory pack as guardrails outside every pricing script, so a configuration mistake can never produce an illegal loan — and the regulator’s returns come out of the same data.

Usual mode
A
Typical products
Short-tenure personal loans · salary-linked loans · Remit-Now-Pay-Later
finOS keeps
Everything — loans, cash and books. OneSphere in Malaysia is designed this way.
Leans on
Regulatory pack · borrower app · eKYC

Wallets & remittance operators

A/B

BNPL, micro-loans, Send-Now-Pay-Later

Credit inside your own flow, with a licensed partner as lender of record. The offer appears in your app or send flow through the widget or the headless API; the loan is booked for the partner, and its books stay in finOS (A) or go to the partner’s accounting system (B).

Usual mode
A or B
Typical products
Buy-now-pay-later · wallet micro-loans · Send-Now-Pay-Later
finOS keeps
The loan; the books in finOS (A) or exported to the partner’s ledger (B).
Leans on
Widgets · headless API · partner programmes

Brokers

B

Margin and securities-backed facilities

Pledged securities are marked to market, and a margin call is a first-class event with the same covenant machinery a corporate loan uses. The broker’s accounting system keeps the books; finOS keeps the facility and the collateral.

Usual mode
B
Typical products
Margin loans · securities-backed credit lines
finOS keeps
The facility and its collateral; summarised journals go to your accounting system.
Leans on
Covenants · collateral · event bridge

What stays the same, and what you choose

Every lender above runs the same product. The parts that protect the money never vary; the parts that make it your business are configuration.

The same for every lender

  • ✓The credit kernel and its ledger — every balance from append-only operations
  • ✓The loan book and the books saved together, in one transaction
  • ✓Decisions that replay exactly, years later, with no LLM in the loop
  • ✓Statutory limits enforced outside every pricing script
  • ✓A hash-chained audit trail and maker-checker on sensitive changes
  • ✓One codebase, upgraded for everyone, supported by Swift

Chosen for your business

  • —Products and pricing · loan types, fees, schedules — flat or reducing balance
  • —Regulatory pack · rate caps, classification, returns, language and calendar per market
  • —Integration mode · A to D: which of the loan, the money and the books finOS keeps
  • —Channels · 01 portal · 02 widget · 03 mobile apps · 04 headless API
  • —Deployment shape · one server, your Kubernetes cluster, or finOS SaaS

How a lender starts

The same four steps whether you are a bank or a moneylender — only the answers differ.

  1. 01

    Fit spike

    A short, time-boxed proof: the ledger pattern and decision latency against your real connectors, before anything is frozen.

  2. 02

    Choose mode and pack

    Decide which records finOS keeps (A–D) and load the regulatory pack for your market.

  3. 03

    Configure products and channels

    Products, pricing, documents and workflows as versioned configuration; pick the channels your borrowers use.

  4. 04

    Prove, then go live

    Dry-run on historical cases and shadow-run on live traffic; nothing goes live unproven.

Any market. Each jurisdiction arrives as a versioned regulatory pack — Nepal and Malaysia first, with Islamic finance as its own pack — over a core that never forks.

You stay the lender of record. Swift provides the software, implements it and supports it. It never lends, never holds funds and never takes credit risk.

Assemble it once. Reshape it as the business changes.

The complete lending business on one configurable platform — the speed of SaaS, the control of your own deployment, and compliance built into the architecture.
