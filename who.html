Who it's for

One lending OS, whatever kind of lender you are

The same kernel and modules serve every shape of lending business. What changes is configuration: the products, the regulatory pack, and which records finOS keeps.

Four ways to connect — the letter on each card

  • AMode A: Standalone · no core banking system
  • BMode B: GL-only · books in your core or ERP
  • CMode C: Full mirror · money stays in your core
  • DMode D: Origination only · your core services the loan

C/D

Any market. Each jurisdiction arrives as a versioned regulatory pack — Nepal and Malaysia first, with Islamic finance as its own pack — over a core that never forks.

You stay the lender of record. Swift provides the software, implements it and supports it. It never lends, never holds funds and never takes credit risk.

Assemble it once. Reshape it as the business changes.

The complete lending business on one configurable platform — the speed of SaaS, the control of your own deployment, and compliance built into the architecture.
