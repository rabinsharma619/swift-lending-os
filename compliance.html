Compliance built into the architecture

Structural, not bolted on

Audit trail, decision reproducibility, statutory limits and data residency are properties of how the platform is built — which is why they cannot be switched off by a configuration mistake.

The central mechanism

Statutory guardrails execute outside policy scripts

A pricing script may propose any rate. The guardrail layer clamps or rejects it — and records that it did.

This is the difference between a platform where compliance is configured and one where it is enforced. If statutory caps lived inside the scripts business users edit, a well-meaning edit could breach a rate ceiling, a tenor limit or a fee prohibition.

Instead they live in a regulatory pack, versioned and effective-dated, evaluated after the script and before the offer. A misconfigured script cannot produce a non-compliant loan.

What guardrails cover

  • Interest and profit-rate ceilings
  • Permitted fees, and prohibitions on all others
  • Default-charge limits and computation basis
  • Product amount caps and tenor limits
  • Mandated payment-allocation order
  • Contact-frequency caps and quiet hours
  • Disclosure and notice requirements

Every clamp is recorded on the offer, so the reason a rate moved is always visible.

The regulator's actual question

Any decision, replayed exactly, years later

"Why was this application declined in March?" is a question the architecture answers directly — not one that starts a project.

Inputs are stored, not re-fetched

Every external call records its request, response, latency and outcome against the subject and the workflow execution. Replay uses stored responses, so no paid provider is called again and the answer cannot drift.

Policy versions are pinned

Every decision records the exact artifact versions in force — scorecard, workflow, pricing script, guardrail pack. Historical evaluation resolves against what was live then.

The rule path is preserved

Which rules fired, on which values, against which thresholds — plus feature contributions where a statistical model contributed, and the human decision and form where one was taken.

Determinism is the enabling property

Because the expression runtime is sandboxed with no ambient clock, no randomness and no I/O, identical recorded inputs produce identical outputs indefinitely. Replay is a guarantee rather than a best effort.

Evidence

Three complementary records — nothing happens off the record

Entity history

What did this loan, application or client look like over time? Every change to state, attributes, status and linked documents, in chronological order.

Nothing is silently overwritten. A payment rollback preserves the original allocation; a manual adjustment preserves the value it changed.

Activity log

Who did what, across which records. Logins, views, approvals, edits, overrides, permission changes.

The surface fraud and security teams use to spot the reviewer working accounts they were never assigned.

Exception log

Where the system itself is failing — integration errors, workflow faults, job failures — with grouping, retry and escalation.

A failed statutory notice becomes an operational incident, not a line in an application log.

Tamper evidence
Audit records are append-only and hash-chained. No application role, including administrator, can alter or delete one.
Configuration is audited too
Changes to products, policy, templates and permissions are themselves logged — so the rules governing audit are as transparent as the rest.
Retention and legal hold
Configurable per record class and jurisdiction, with legal hold suspending deletion.
Multi-market by construction

Regulatory packs, not forks

The core stays market-agnostic. Everything jurisdiction-specific lives in a versioned, effective-dated pack.

What a pack carries

  • Statutory guardrail rules and their thresholds
  • Prescribed agreement and disclosure templates
  • Rate and APR calculation conventions
  • Bureau reporting formats and cadence
  • Supervisory return definitions
  • Collections conduct rules — contact caps, quiet hours, notice periods
  • Provisioning and classification inputs

Why this shape

A lender entering a second market should be running a configuration exercise, not a second implementation. Because packs are versioned and effective-dated, a historical evaluation resolves against the pack that was in force at the time — which is what makes a retrospective audit tractable.

And because no jurisdiction rule is hard-coded in the core, adding a market never destabilises an existing one.

Depth, not checkbox coverage

Regulatory packs are researched against primary sources — the statute and the regulator's own published documents — rather than assembled from summaries. That research routinely surfaces findings that change a product design before it is built. An example →

Own your platform, own your data

Access, encryption and residency

Role and attribute-based access

Granular permissions per entity and action, plus attribute rules for thresholds, segregation of duties, four-eyes approval and time-boxed break-glass access that raises an alert.

Data scoping in the database

Visibility limits by branch, region, product or portfolio are enforced by row-level security — not by application code that can be bypassed.

Encryption everywhere

In transit and at rest, with field-level encryption for the most sensitive identifiers and keys held in a managed store, never beside the data they protect.

PII classification drives behaviour

Fields are classified once; masking, log suppression, export control and retention then follow automatically rather than by reviewer discipline.

Residency is a deployment property

Single-tenant by default, deployed where the institution requires — including fully air-gapped, with no outbound dependency for runtime or licensing.

Erasure without losing the record

Subject erasure is honoured by crypto-shredding personal identifiers while preserving the financial and audit record the law requires. Row deletion is never the mechanism.

Islamic finance

Shariah compliance is a ledger property, not a label

The most damaging failure in this domain is a system that computes interest internally and renames it "profit" at the presentation layer.

Under a conventional loan the balance grows as interest accrues with time. Under a compliant sale-based contract the customer owes a fixed sale price from inception — profit is recognised over time for accounting, but nothing accrues. That difference reaches the ledger, so it is modelled in the kernel through a contract-type abstraction rather than a product setting.

What the kernel models natively

  • Profit recognition as an accounting entry that does not change the customer's obligation
  • Compensation and penalty separated — penalty amounts routed to a charity payable, never recognised as income
  • Rebate on early settlement as a first-class operation, applied on restructure and acceleration too
  • Purification tracking for non-permissible income
  • Non-compliance event register, distinct from the exception log
Stated honestly

Contract permissibility varies by jurisdiction and by scholar — a structure widely accepted in one market may be restricted in another. Product structures and market-specific rules require confirmation by a qualified Shariah adviser and local counsel. What the platform provides is a ledger that can model them correctly, and the governance surfaces to evidence it.

In short

Compliance you can demonstrate, not just assert

Guardrails that configuration cannot breach. Decisions that replay exactly. An audit trail no role can alter. Regulatory packs instead of forks. Residency as a deployment choice.
