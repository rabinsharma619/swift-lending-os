The Building Platform

Assemble the exact workflow your business runs on

Prebuilt lending modules over one credit kernel — composed into your product, then reshaped as products, markets and regulations evolve.

At the centre

One credit kernel, and the modules that compose on it

The kernel owns what must be correct. The modules and configuration own what must be fast to change.

Most lending systems bundle both, which is why they ossify — the loan ledger and a seasonal promotion end up in the same deployment unit, and every change carries the risk of the most dangerous one.

Swift Lending OS separates them permanently. Credit operations, balances, schedules and payment allocation live in the kernel. Products, pricing, credit policy, documents and communications are configuration on top of it.

The credit kernel

Every balance change is a discrete, timestamped, reversible, audited operation. No service anywhere writes a balance directly.

  • Credit operations — accrual, charge, payment, past-due, and custom types
  • Balances and payment schedules
  • Payment allocation across obligations
  • Contract types — conventional and Islamic sale-based
  • Ledger audit trail

Any loan's position at any past instant reconstructs from its operation history alone.

Modular core

Connected workflows, unified data

Separate modules over one data model — so the loan a decision created is the loan servicing bills and collections works.

01

Origination

A repeatable, auditable path from first touch to funded loan. Multi-channel intake, duplicate detection, KYC/AML/fraud orchestration, credit assessment, profile building, offer generation, document collection and generation, e-signature, approval authority and disbursement.

Most applications flow through untouched; underwriters see only what needs judgment.

02

Decisioning

Scorecards as first-class objects, decision tables and trees, one sandboxed expression runtime across every scope, and a feature store turning raw provider payloads into named business variables.

Dry run, replay against historical cohorts, shadow-run on live traffic and champion/challenger — before any policy takes effect.

03

Products & pricing

A two-tier product and variant model, so segment, channel and promotional variations are configuration rather than duplicated products. Pricing scripts read verified profiles; multiple payment-calculation engines; collateral rules and per-product document requirements.

A new segment variant goes live in hours, without engineering.

04

Servicing

Live loans maintain themselves. Accrual, charge, payment and past-due operations run on schedule; delinquency ages automatically; restructures, payment holidays, forbearance, payoff quotes, early settlement and write-off are permissioned, validated actions.

Staff act on exceptions, not routine — and every intervention is in the audit trail.

05

Payments

Money is captured at the client level and allocated to loans separately — so one transfer covering three loans is one entry, not three. Smart allocation by configured strategy, directed allocation when instructed, clean rollback that preserves history, and continuous reconciliation.

Rails, gateway callbacks and bank files all land in the same transaction model.

06

Collections & recovery

A strategy engine that segments the past-due book and selects the next best action. Contact-frequency caps and quiet hours are enforced by the platform, not left to agent discipline. Promise-to-pay, hardship and arrangements, charge-off, recoveries and agency placement.

Vulnerability flags and representation suppress standard cadences automatically.

07

Covenants & monitoring

Contractual conditions that outlast origination — ratio ceilings, coverage floors, insurance currency, review cadence — modelled as a metric, a specification and a baseline anchored at signing.

Breach is a first-class event on the loan, in the queue and in reporting. Not a rediscovery at the next manual review.

08

Analytics & reporting

Operational views on live data, portfolio and vintage dashboards on a modelled reporting replica, regulatory returns generated from the operational record, and provisioning inputs for impairment models.

Analyst queries never contend with origination latency.

Running beneath all of them

Documents and communications with versioned templates and e-signature; the connector layer and public API; role and attribute-based access control; and the audit trio — entity history, activity log and exception log.

How it reshapes

Three layers, one rule between them

SDK — engineers
Entities, business processes and their state machines, events, actions, custom credit operations, connectors, scheduled jobs. Ordinary framework code in your repository, through your review and release pipeline.
Configuration — the business
Products and variants, pricing scripts, decision workflows and scorecards, feature mappings, metrics and covenants, document and notification templates, decision forms, roles, queues and catalogs. Versioned, diffable, promotable, reversible artifacts.
Operations — your teams
Applications and manual decisions, loan tracking, transactions and allocation, collections queues, covenant monitoring, dashboards and exception triage.
The rule

Every configuration option exists because an SDK primitive enables it. When the business needs something the configuration layer cannot express, engineering adds a primitive — and it appears as a new configuration option rather than a bespoke feature.

What that means in practice

New segment variant

Hours. Configuration only.

New pricing rule

Days, with replay evidence attached.

New data provider

A small connector, then configuration forever after.

New market

A regulatory pack — not a fork.

Design

Three ideas that make the modules composable

Process and decision are separate

The business process owns stages and transitions. The decision workflow owns the verdict at a transition, emitting facts — proceed, pause for a human, or hard stop — and exiting immediately.

Credit policy and journey shape then change independently, owned by different teams on different cadences.

Every entity has its own state machine

On one deal, the borrower can be in income verification while a co-borrower is approved and collateral awaits valuation.

Concurrency instead of a serial queue — and re-doing one piece never resets the others.

Profiles sit between assessment and pricing

Workflows and reviewers write verified values into a typed profile. Pricing reads profiles, never raw provider data.

So what you know about an applicant and how you price it change without colliding.

Boundaries

What the platform deliberately leaves to others

A platform earns trust by being clear about its edges.

Not a core banking ledger

We post to your ledger of record. That is a different correctness domain.

Not your consumer brand

APIs, SDK and reference surfaces. The customer experience stays yours.

Not a credit bureau

We integrate bureau and alternative data. We do not sell it.

Not a CRM

We emit events. Your relationship tooling consumes them.
